EcologicalCellularAutomata.jl

A model zoo of published ecological cellular automata, from plant diseases and agricultural and forest pests to coastal dunes, each runnable on any compatible CellularAutomataCore.jl backend.

Part of the ecorecipes cellular-automata ecosystem: CellularAutomataCore.jl → CategoricalCellularAutomata.jl (applied category theory) and CellularAutomataCore.jl → EcologicalCellularAutomata.jl (model zoo).

Each model reimplements a published cellular automaton for a plant disease, a vector of plant disease, an agricultural or forest pest, or a landform shaped by vegetation (bio-geomorphology), as a backend-agnostic CellularAutomataCore model, together with metadata recording the citation, the mechanism, the validation reported by the source, and how faithfully the reimplementation follows it. The literature these models come from is reviewed in (Tonnang et al., 2017) among others.

A reproduction implements the source's model with its parameters; an adaptation fills gaps or departs from it in ways its metadata lists. Each model's vignette compares it with the source's results and states its deviations.

using EcologicalCellularAutomata, CellularAutomataCore
list_models()
model_info(:fungus_dispersal)                 # citation, fidelity, deviations, ...
sol = solve(problem(:fungus_dispersal; seed=1))
model = build(:fungus_dispersal)              # the CAModel, for any scheme and backend

Some sources update individuals one at a time or need decisions on the whole lattice (spraying when a pest passes a control level, spores walking between patches). Those parts are callbacks stored with the problem, so solve runs them, drawing their random numbers from the problem's seed.
