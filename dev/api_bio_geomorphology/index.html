Bio-geomorphology

Builders, problem constructors and helpers of the models whose category is "bio-geomorphology": landforms shaped by vegetation.

Coastal dunes

EcologicalCellularAutomata.build_dune_formation — Function
build_dune_formation() -> CAModel

The beach-dune model of (Teixeira et al., 2023) (DuBeVeg). Each cell of 1 m holds an elevation in slabs of height h (topo), the cover of a pioneer and of a stabilizing plant (pioneer, stabilizer; their sum, at most 1, is the vegetation effectiveness ρ), the slabs gained or lost this year (balance), the elevation of the reference beach surface (zref), and the probability that the sea has reset the cell this year (sea). Rows run cross-shore, from the sea at the first column to the land at the last; the wind blows landward. The dynamics are dune_step!, a callback stored with the problem.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.problem_dune_formation — Function
problem_dune_formation(; scenario=:wide, representation=:saltation, alongshore=100,
                       years=50, seed=0, pmap=(;), kwargs...) -> CAProblem

The setup of (Teixeira et al., 2023) for its :narrow (100 m) or :wide (500 m) beach (see dune_profile), with alongshore rows of 1 m, for years years of 50 aeolian iterations. representation = :saltation is the source's change (slabs of 0.02 m hopping 5 cells, trapped part-way by vegetation of at least 0.3); :default is DuBeVeg as it was (slabs of 0.1 m hopping 1 cell). Plants cover the foredune fully above 6 m and with a random cover between 4 and 6 m, pioneers on its seaward side and stabilizers behind its crest (the source does not say which). Water levels come from dune_water_levels, drawn from seed.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_step! — Function
dune_step!(u, p, t, rng) -> Int

One aeolian iteration t of (Teixeira et al., 2023), applied to the state u in place: the shadow zones and the probabilities of erosion and deposition are fixed from the state at its start; every cell above the groundwater surface (G times the reference surface) and above sea level, outside the shadow, gives up a slab with probability (1 - ρ) pe0; each slab moves landward a cell at a time, landing at the end of every hop of L cells with probability ρ (1 - pd0) + pd0 (1 in the shadow) and, part-way through a hop, with the same probability where ρ ≥ rho_star (never otherwise); slabs leaving the land side are lost. Avalanches then restore the angles of repose. Every hydro_every iterations the sea acts with the next water level of water_levels plus wave run-up (dune_runup), followed by avalanches; every iterations iterations the plants grow, and their cover is then multiplied by the chance that the sea did not reset the cell during the year, as in DuBeVeg. Returns the slabs that crossed the dune toe (the first cell above 3 m) in all rows.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_callback — Function
dune_callback() -> DiscreteCallback

A callback that runs dune_step! after each step, with a random number generator seeded from the problem's seed and the iteration.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_runup — Function
dune_runup(η, slope) -> Float64

The wave run-up (m) above a water level η (m) on a beach of gradient slope, by Stockdon et al.'s relation as DuBeVeg applies it, with the wave height and length correlated with the water level: H = max(0, 2.931 η - 2.637), L = max(0, 46.74 η - 30.59).

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_profile — Function
dune_profile(; beach=500, offshore=60, toe=2.5, h=0.02) -> NamedTuple

The source's initial cross-shore profile, in metres on 1 m cells from the sea to the land: a Dean profile below sea level over offshore m (to about 1.8 m deep), a beach rising linearly from 0 at the shoreline to toe m at the foot of the dune over beach m (2.5 m, as the source's figure shows; its text says 3 m), a foredune ridge 100 m wide rising to 15 m (a smooth bump, its shape read from the source's figure), and a 5 m plateau behind it. Returns the elevation and the reference surface (the beach without the dune, level under it), both in slabs of height h.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_water_levels — Function
dune_water_levels(rng; years=50) -> Vector{Float64}

A synthetic series of the highest water level (m above mean sea level) in each spring-neap cycle, 25 a year, standing in for the source's observed levels at Scheveningen: 1 m plus a Gumbel-distributed surge of scale 0.3 m.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_toe — Function
dune_toe(u, r; level=3, h=0.02) -> Int

The source's dune toe of row r: the most seaward column whose elevation is above level m, with slabs of height h, or one past the last column if none is.

EcologicalCellularAutomata.dune_measures — Function
dune_measures(u; h=0.02) -> NamedTuple

The source's measures of the foredune, averaged over the rows: its volume (m³ per m alongshore, above 3 m), its width (m, from the dune toe to the land side) and the position of its toe (the column of the first cell above 3 m), with slabs of height h.
